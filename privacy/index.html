Privacy Policy

Privacy, plainly put.

Last updated: August 20, 2026

Overview

Stash is a local clipboard manager for macOS. It has no accounts, advertising, analytics, cloud sync, or remote servers. Stash does not transmit your clipboard history, screenshots, or other content to the developer or to third parties.

Clipboard data

When clipboard history is enabled, Stash stores content that you copy on your Mac, such as text, links, images, screenshots, and file references. This lets you search, organize, copy, preview, and delete recent items. You can pause clipboard history, exclude specific apps, clear individual items, or clear all history in Settings.

Screen capture and screenshots

Stash does not record continuous screen video or screen audio.

If you choose to use Stash’s screenshot features, Stash may process: (1) the image created when you explicitly invoke the region-screenshot shortcut; and (2) new screenshot image files in the screenshot folder only when you explicitly enable screenshot monitoring.

Stash uses this information solely to make screenshots available in your local clipboard history. It does not use screenshot data for analytics, advertising, profiling, training, or any purpose unrelated to the screenshot features.

Screenshot data is not transmitted, uploaded, sold, or shared with the developer or any third party. It is stored only on your Mac in Stash’s local application container, together with a local thumbnail and history metadata.

Retention and deletion

You control how long Stash keeps clipboard history in Settings. You can delete individual items or clear all history at any time. Unpinned images and screenshots are removed according to the retention period you select. Pinned items remain until you delete them.

Third parties

Stash does not share personal data, clipboard data, or screenshot data with third parties. The app does not use third-party analytics, advertising, authentication, payment, AI, or data-processing services.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the Stash developer through the support contact listed on the App Store product page.
